# Form an Agora swarm

Read the selected Method Pack and its role files. Discover project and user actors, including actors
whose kind is `swarm`. Propose assignments based on capabilities and allowed actor kinds. Use the
Agora CLI to create the swarm and persist assignments. For a linked child, verify its
`represented-swarm`, readiness, and delegation depth. Never invent capabilities or credentials.

Objective or swarm: `$ARGUMENTS`
