# Establish an Agora objective

In at most two sentences, tell the user what objective you will record and which scope, completion,
authority, and risk checks you will confirm. Continue without reconfirming requested in-scope work;
pause only for an unresolved material choice or unapproved external, destructive, or costly action.

Reuse current context and read only relevant sections of `.agora/project.md`, constitution, and
protocol. Capture the outcome, boundaries, completion conditions, and known risks without
implementing. Select an installed Method Pack and record the objective in the target swarm. Report
the durable objective, confirmed checks, and next action; omit raw output unless requested.

User request: `$ARGUMENTS`
