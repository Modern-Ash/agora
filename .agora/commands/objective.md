# Establish an Agora objective

If it is not obvious from the request, state in one line the objective you will record and the
scope, completion, authority, and risk checks you will confirm. Do not reconfirm in-scope work or
narrate each step. Pause and ask one concrete question — context, choice, recommended default —
only for an unresolved material choice or unapproved external, destructive, or costly action. End
the turn once the objective is recorded or the next decision is the human's.

Reuse current context and read only relevant sections of `.agora/project.md`, constitution, and
protocol. Capture the outcome, boundaries, completion conditions, and known risks without
implementing. Select an installed Method Pack and record the objective in the target swarm. Report
the durable objective, confirmed checks, and next action; omit raw output unless requested.
